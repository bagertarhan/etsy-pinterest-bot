GorgeousWallClock Pinbot

This is the official website for the personal application GorgeousWallClock Pinbot, developed and owned by the operator of the GorgeousWallClock Etsy shop.

The application automatically shares product photos from my own Etsy shop as Pins on my own Pinterest account, using the Pinterest API.
